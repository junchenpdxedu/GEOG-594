Slide 1 Graduate Term Project

  • Can partner or go solo on project
  • Equal effort if partnered
  • Extra Credit opportunity for undergrads

Slide 2 Term Project: Purpose

  • The purpose of this assignment is to help students develop effective communication skills while gaining practical experience with GIS applications for water resources.
  • The labs and short assignments will prepare you to determine which types of analysis will help you solve water issues with GIS.

Slide 4 Term Project: Due Dates

  • Project Proposal – Nov 1st
  • Annotated Bibliography – Nov 22nd
  • Report – Dec 11th
  • *No late submission accepted for term project work

Slide 5 Term Project: Teams and Topics

  • Individual or groups of 2
  • Topics to study water-related problems using GIS
  • Topics approved by the instructor
  • Groups clarify individual roles
  • An undergrad can pair with a grad for 10% EC (with consent, of course). Undergrad is expected to perform equally.

Slide 6 Term Project – Example Topic Ideas

  • Flood/disaster management mapping / scenarios / preparedness
  • Lidar-based inundation mapping along a gaged tributary
  • Test and validate a wetland identification model (WIM Model)
  • An analysis of slope stability or erosion potential on Mount _________
  • Glacial volume calculations and future projections
  • Stream temperature modeling
  • Stormwater and pollution mitigation in urban environments
  • Hydrology and debris flow relations
  • An analysis of riparian conditions and water quality
  • Dam raise scenarios for _______ Lake
  • Minimum eroded volume from a forested watershed
  • Aquifer storage and contamination
  • Comparisons of terrain types and hydrologic characteristics
  • Reservoir contents table from bathymetric survey data
  • Groundwater model in _______ basin
  • Sediment loading in ________ Bay
  • Geomorphic characteristics of _______ watershed

Lecture notes

Or any novel techniques for analysis and cartography of water resources

Slide 7 Term Project: Brainstorming a Topic

  • Brainstorming Project Topic
    • I highly recommend you reach out to me via email to set up a time and talk about the feasibility of your topic before you submit the proposal

Slide 8 Term Project: Proposal

  • Project Proposal (2–3 pages)
    • Objective – What are you investigating?
    • Background – Has this been done before? Why does this research matter?
    • Approach – How do you plan on resolving the problem? What data do you need?
    • Deliverables – What are your final products? Map? GIS datasets? Report? (yes)
    • Budget/timeline – How long do you expect it will take? Billable hours

Slide 9 Term Project: Annotated Bibliography

  • Annotated Bibliography
    • Minimum 10 references
    • 4–6 sentences describing what the article describes and how it relates to your research
    • Appropriate citation (author, year, title, journal name, volume, page) – more details to follow

Slide 10 Term Project: Final Report

  • Report (~10 pages)
    • Format requirements (MLA or PSU Graduate Thesis template!)
    • 3,000–5,000 words
    • 3–10 figures
    • Contains: Abstract, Introduction, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, References Cited, Billable hours breakdown
    • 100 Points possible – rubric to come

Slide 11 Term Project: Evaluation and Example

  • Evaluation / grading rubric: See Syllabus
  • Example: Oregon Lakeshed Terrain Roughness: Terrain and hydrologic analysis for lakeshed delineation and investigation of the relationships between precipitation, stream system density, and terrain roughness in Oregon.
