GIS for Water Resources

Instructor: Junjie Chen, PhD, PH · junchen@pdx.edu

Weekly lectures

Week 1

  1. Lecture 1a: Introduction Course orientation, data models, vector and raster data, hydrology vs. hydrography, and GIS data sources.

Week 2

  1. Lecture 1b: Cartographic Principles Visual contrast, legibility, figure-ground, hierarchy and layout balance, with example maps.
  2. Lecture 2a: Graduate Term Project Teams, topics, due dates, and what goes in the proposal, annotated bibliography and report.
  3. Lecture 2b: Example Term Project A past graduate project: Oregon lakeshed terrain roughness, drainage density and precipitation.
  4. Lecture 2c: Tapered Streams 101 Step-by-step ArcGIS Pro demo: vary stream line widths by flow using NHDPlus HR.
